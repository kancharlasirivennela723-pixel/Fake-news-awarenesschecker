Fake News Awareness Checker Website 🌐
1. Project Introduction
A Fake News Awareness Checker is a website that helps people identify potentially fake news and misleading information on the internet. Nowadays, fake news spreads quickly through WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, and other social media platforms.
Our website helps users check news claims, learn how to verify information, and become more aware before sharing news with others.
Main aim: Stop the spread of misinformation and encourage people to share only verified information.
2. How Does Our Website Work?
Step 1: Enter News
The user enters a news headline or a claim they want to check.
Step 2: Check the Claim
The website searches trusted sources or uses a simple checking system to look for supporting evidence.
Step 3: Show the Result
The website displays a result such as “Verified,” “Possibly Misleading,” or “Not Enough Evidence.”
Step 4: Spread Awareness
Users learn tips for identifying fake news before sharing it with friends and family.
Important: The website should not label news as fake just because it cannot find evidence. Results should include sources and explain uncertainty.
3. Pros (Advantages)
Prevents misinformation: Helps users identify suspicious news.
Saves time: Makes it easier to start checking a news claim.
Creates awareness: Teaches people to think critically.
Protects society: Can reduce the spread of rumours and misleading messages.
Easy to use: Students and ordinary users can check claims through a simple interface.
